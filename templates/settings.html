When visiting a subdomain with many URLs, they will open in batches of this size to prevent browser crashes.

ℹ️ How Batch Opening Works

  • When you visit a subdomain, only the first batch of URLs will open automatically
  • A progress screen will show how many URLs have opened (e.g., "10 / 47 URLs")
  • Click "Next Batch" to open the next set of URLs
  • Or click "Open All Remaining" to open everything at once
  • This setting applies to all users and all subdomains
